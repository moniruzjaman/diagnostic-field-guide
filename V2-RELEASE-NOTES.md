# v2.0.0 — Field Diagnostic Workbench

This release adds a guided, offline-capable field case workflow alongside the translated CABI reference book and interactive dashboard.

- Structured crop, growth-stage, field-location and observation record.
- Symptom and affected-plant-part checklist; field distribution, onset and environment.
- Transparent matching across seven broad cause groups: pest/mite, fungal, bacterial, virus/phytoplasma-like symptoms, nutrition/soil chemistry, water/root, and weather/other abiotic stress.
- Results show selected evidence and field verification prompts. Scores are evidence-match counts, not probabilities or diagnoses.
- Verification checklist, management notes, follow-up date, on-device case storage, JSON export and print/PDF.
- No account, API, image upload or remote case storage. Records stay in browser local storage unless exported.

## Safety and scope
The workbench does not name a pathogen, prescribe a pesticide, or replace field/laboratory diagnosis. Similar symptoms may have multiple causes and mixed causes can occur. Verify evidence and consult local extension/research specialists before high-impact decisions. Follow current Bangladesh registration, product label, crop/pest, dose, pre-harvest interval and resistance-management guidance for pesticide use.

The reference ebook remains the Bangla adaptation of CABI's *Plantwise Diagnostic Field Guide* (2015), under the stated CC BY-NC terms. The new workbench is an original educational companion and does not change the source book or its image credits.
